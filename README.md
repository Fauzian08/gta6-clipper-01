## The moment

IShowSpeed's final mission in GTA 6, from his own livestream VOD ("BEATING GTA 6 RIGHT NOW 100% (FINAL MISSION)", 20 Nov 2026). After 52 hours straight without sleeping, Speed refuses to split Jason and Lucia apart — they choose to swim for it together, like Bonnie and Clyde. Instead both are gunned down on the bridge, and Speed is left in shock as the credits roll.

- 05:11:19 — the final standoff on the bridge: "Get out of the vehicle with your hands in the air!"
- 05:12:00 — Jason: "If we can get into the water, we swim for it."
- 05:12:29–05:12:50 — the last kiss, Jason steps out with his gun, police open fire, both die
- 05:14:29–05:14:33 — Speed's reaction, hands over his mouth, in disbelief

The clip (59 seconds, 1080x1920) opens with the hook — "Played GTA 6 for 52 hours straight without sleeping... only to end up with the most heartbreaking ending" — then the moment itself, and closes with the make.fun pitch: make clips like this with AI and earn money on make.fun.

## The video

`VIDEO.json` at this repository's root holds the video's address. The video is public on YouTube as a Short, and its description carries the bounty's line and the play code `makefun-minnow-windsock-mole`.

## Run it locally

    mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts

`out/VIDEO.json` is then byte for byte this directory's `VIDEO.json`.
